About Alleana

Discover how AIleana transforms everyday social interactions
into income opportunities

AIleana enables creators and freelancers to earn directly from their work through subscriptions, digital sales, service bookings, licensing, and commissions. Monetization is built into the platform, not dependent on ads, brand deals, or external tools.

Creators retain full ownership of their content and intellectual property. Smart licensing and royalty systems ensure you earn whenever your work is sold, reused, or distributed, with transparent tracking and automated payouts.

Freelancers access verified job listings and project opportunities, intelligently matched by AIVA based on skills, experience, and reputation. No fake gigs, no race-to-the-bottom bidding, no hidden middlemen.
